# Troubleshooting

## A seller shows no listings, or a page looks empty

First check the date range at the top of the page. Listings pages default to the last 120 days, so anything listed earlier will not appear until you widen the range and click Apply.

If the range is right and the page is still empty, the seller's connection to eBay may have expired. eBay access is granted for a limited period and has to be renewed by authorizing again. Go to the "add sellers" page and click "authorize through eBay login" for that seller — re-authorizing an existing seller is supported and refreshes the connection without creating a duplicate.

## I added a seller but nothing happened

Watch for the notification at the top of the "add sellers" page. It names the seller that was authorized on success, or explains what went wrong. If the eBay popup closes with no message, the authorization did not complete — click authorize and finish the eBay sign-in without closing the window.

If your browser blocks popups, allow them for this site and try again.

## My inbox is empty

Buyer messages only arrive if the seller is subscribed to a notification topic. Open the Notifications page, select the seller, and subscribe to the message topic. New messages arrive from that point on; subscribing does not backfill messages that were sent earlier.

## The numbers on Payouts don't match my sales

Payouts are the money eBay actually transferred to your bank, after fees, refunds and any holds. Sales totals on the Listings or Charts pages are the value of the items themselves. The two will not match, and the gap is normal.

## I forgot my password

Use the "Forgot password?" link on the sign-in page. Sealift emails you a link that lets you set a new one. The link is single use and expires after one hour. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you used the address you registered with.

## I need to change my eBay keys

Open Settings. You can update the App ID, Dev ID, Cert ID, RuName and the Sandbox setting there. This is what to do if you rotate your eBay Cert ID, or if you mistyped a value when you registered. After changing the keys you may need to authorize your sellers again.

## Sandbox vs production

If you ticked Sandbox when registering, Sealift talks to eBay's test environment, and every figure you see is fake test data. You can switch this in Settings. Sandbox and production use different eBay keysets, so switching usually means updating your keys too.

## The AI assistant says it doesn't know

The assistant answers from Sealift's documentation. If it does not know something, the documentation likely does not cover it. It also cannot see your account data — it cannot tell you your balance, your sales total, or what a specific buyer said.

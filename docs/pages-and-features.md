# Pages in Sealift

The left navigation groups pages into a few top-level links plus two dropdowns, Analytics and More.

## Inbox

Buyer messages and other eBay notifications arrive here in real time, without needing to refresh. Each message can be marked as read, moved to Trash, or deleted permanently from Trash. Inbox and Trash are two tabs on the same page.

Messages only arrive for sellers you have subscribed to notifications for — see the Notifications page. If your inbox is empty and you expect messages, check that the seller has an active subscription there.

## Create Listing

Upload or drag in a photo of an item, and Sealift uses AI vision to draft listing copy for it — a short description written in a plain, eBay-style tone. You can edit the generated text, add a title and price, and use the Regenerate button to try a different wording for the same photo. The Copy button puts the whole listing on your clipboard so you can paste it into eBay or anywhere else.

The photo is only used to generate the description. Sealift does not create the listing on eBay for you.

## Gallery

A visual grid of your listings, showing each item's photo, title and price. Useful for spotting listings with missing or poor images. It uses the same date range and filters as the Listings page.

## Analytics → Listings

A table of listings for each seller, with the date range, status filter (Active, Completed, Ended, or all), and page size at the top. Set a From and To date and click Apply to load a different period. Because eBay limits how much history one request can cover, Sealift automatically splits long ranges into shorter periods and merges the results.

## Analytics → Payouts

Money eBay has actually paid out to each seller, with the payout amount, status and date. Payouts are what landed in your bank account, which is not the same as your sales total — eBay fees, refunds and holds are already taken out.

## Analytics → Charts

A line chart per seller comparing the cumulative value of items listed against the cumulative value of payouts received, over a range you choose. It is meant for spotting trends, not for accounting.

## More → Accounts

Your eBay account summary per seller: current balance, last invoice amount, any past-due amount, and the payment method on file. This is eBay's billing view of your seller account.

## More → Transactions

Transaction summaries per seller — counts and amounts for credits, debits, amounts on hold, and items still processing.

## More → Notifications

Where you subscribe each seller to eBay notification topics, such as new buyer messages. Subscribing here is what makes messages appear in the Inbox. You can also unsubscribe a seller from a topic on this page.

## More → Changelog

A history of notable changes to Sealift.

## More → Settings

Change your password and update the eBay developer keys attached to your account.

## More → Admin

The danger zone: permanently delete your Sealift account and everything in it.

## The AI assistant

The chat panel opens from the tab on the right-hand edge of any page (or the floating button on mobile). Ask it how something in Sealift works. It answers from Sealift's documentation and shows which documents it used. It cannot see your listings, payouts or balances — for those, use the pages above.
